### 🔬 Research interests

- **Shared experience & language**: how watching together shapes conversation and memory
- **Decision-making & social interaction**: strategies under changing power, modeled with RL and clustering
- **Dynamic structure of human data**: temporal and sequential patterns in behavior and speech

### 🧪 Current work

- **CHEN Lab, NTU** (Undergraduate Research Assistant, 2025 to present): Python workflows for language, behavioral, time-series, and multimodal data; dyadic interaction and VR studies with BIOPAC
- **NSTC Undergraduate Research Project** (2026 to present): *Shared Viewing and Semantic Structure in Memory*, using LLM and semantic-representation analyses of free recall
- **Complex Human Data Hub, Melbourne** (Research Intern, 2026): Chinese and English semantic spaces from word-association data and cross-lingual alignment

### 🎤 Selected presentations

- *From watching together to thinking alike: Semantic convergence in shared experience.* TPA 2025, oral presentation
- *Incentives shape how the powerful and the powerless behave as power becomes equal.* TPA 2026
